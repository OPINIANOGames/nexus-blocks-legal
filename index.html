1. Privacy Policy

OPINIANO Games ("we", "our", or "us") built the mobile game NEXUS BLOCKS (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your information.

1.1 Information We Collect & Third-Party SDKs

  • Leaderboard & Profile Data (LootLocker): We use LootLocker to manage All-Time and Monthly leaderboards (all_time_level, monthly_score). When you play, an anonymous guest session identifier, your chosen player display name, equipped badge/skin metadata, and high scores/levels are transmitted to LootLocker to display global rankings.
  • Rewarded Advertisements (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may automatically collect your device's Advertising ID (AAID), coarse location (country/region based on IP), device model, and ad interaction diagnostic data for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): All financial transactions for Golds and Shards (gold_tier_1–gold_tier_6 and shard_tier_1–shard_tier_6) are processed exclusively by Google Play. We never view, collect, or store your credit card, GCash, Maya, or banking details. We only receive an encrypted purchase token and order ID from Google to verify receipts and credit your in-game currency.
  • Local Save Data: Game settings, unlocked backgrounds, journeys, skins, and currency balances are stored on your device.

1.2 How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly to maintain global and monthly leaderboards, verify In-App Purchase receipts, deliver optional rewarded ad bonuses, prevent score or currency tampering, and fix technical bugs.

1.3 Third-Party Privacy Policies

We do not sell your personal data. Data processed by our service providers is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • LootLocker Privacy Policy

1.4 Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
